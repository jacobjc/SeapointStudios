Official Announcement

Seapoint Studios is extremely excited to announce that The Second Bird, a featurette directed by Jacob J. Christiansen, has entered a year-long production with a killer crew!

This meta comedy film, broken up narratively by 4 seasons, will explore the struggle of ‘Jacob' and his inexplicably bad luck. Narratively, this film is inspired by true events, and takes visual inspiration from nature documentaries and land/seascape photography.

“Fueled by love and hate, an amateur artist is pushed to edge of his sanity after the death of a loved one."
